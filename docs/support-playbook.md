## 2. Core Support Principles

Support agents must strictly adhere to these security and operational guidelines during all user interactions:

1.  **Never Request Secrets:** Never ask users to send credentials, API keys, passwords, database URLs, or Stripe tokens.
2.  **Protect Payment Data:** Never request full credit card numbers, CVVs, or bank details. Redirect payment issues to the secure Customer Portal.
3.  **Validate Ownership:** Verify a user's identity (account email or ownership cookie context) before discussing or altering database entries or result reports.
4.  **No AI Quality Guarantees:** Remind users that AI analysis output is generated by third-party model providers, is provided "as-is," and is not guaranteed to be free of errors, hallucinations, or inaccuracies.
5.  **No Internal Metadata Leaks:** Do not share database UUIDs, system logs, provider configurations, or serverless function details with users.

## 3. Common User Issues & Response Templates

### Template 01: "I lost access to my anonymous result" / "I cleared cookies and cannot see my report"
> **Subject:** Odzyskanie dostępu do raportu / Recovering access to your report
>
> **PL:**  
> Dzień dobry,  
> Dostęp do raportów wygenerowanych bez rejestracji konta (anonimowych) jest zapisywany w plikach cookie Państwa przeglądarki. Wyczyszczenie pamięci podręcznej przeglądarki, plików cookie lub korzystanie z trybu incognito powoduje utratę tego powiązania. Ze względów bezpieczeństwa nie mamy technicznej możliwości ręcznego przywrócenia dostępu do raportów anonimowych bez potwierdzenia własności. Zalecamy rejestrację bezpłatnego konta, co pozwoli na trwałe zapisywanie historii analiz w panelu użytkownika.
>
> **EN:**  
> Hello,  
> Access to reports generated without registering an account (anonymous audits) is stored using your browser's cookies. Clearing cookies, browser cache, or using incognito mode removes this ownership link. For security reasons, we cannot manually restore access to anonymous results without verification. We highly recommend registering a free account to automatically save your audit history in your user dashboard.

## 4. Billing Support Rules

*   **No Refund Promises:** Support agents must never promise or guarantee a refund to a user. Final approval is subject to verifying consumption limits (<10 audits) and age restrictions (<14 days).
*   **No Manual Card Processing:** Support agents are prohibited from manually entering credit card numbers in Stripe. Users must execute all transactions via Stripe Checkout.
*   **Redirect to Portal:** Direct users to perform cancellations or invoice downloads via the Stripe Customer Portal.

---

## 5. Privacy Support Rules

*   **Ownership Check:** Always verify ownership before deleting records or changing subscription states.
*   **Anonymous Cookie Warning:** Inform anonymous users that clearing cookies or changing devices breaks ownership association permanently.
*   **Legal Disclaimer:** Support replies concerning terms, privacy policies, or GDPR rights are for general guidance and do **not** constitute formal legal advice.

## 7. What Not to Say (Prohibited Content)

Support staff must **strictly avoid** using these phrases or claims:

*   *Prohibited:* "Our application is 100% GDPR compliant and certified."  
    *Instead use:* "We process customer data in accordance with our draft privacy policy guidelines."
*   *Prohibited:* "We never store any prompts or user data."  
    *Instead use:* "We retain user history based on the periods disclosed in our privacy drafts."
*   *Prohibited:* "The AI output is guaranteed to be error-free and highly accurate."  
    *Instead use:* "AI outputs are suggestions and must be verified by the user."
*   *Prohibited:* "Your refund is guaranteed under all circumstances."  
    *Instead use:* "Refunds are processed in accordance with our refund policy guidelines."
*   *Prohibited:* "Our AI runtime provider is OpenRouter."  
    *Instead use:* "Our platform integrates with the Google Gemini API."
*   Sending official communications from `support@promptpolish.com` or `privacy@promptpolish.com` before the mailboxes are verified active.
